# Sound credits

Every sound in this folder is a real recording, trimmed to its clearest few
seconds, loudness-normalized, faded in and out, and re-encoded as mono MP3. A
few short calls (pig, seal, chick) are repeated once, and the dolphin and
giraffe clips are filtered to cut background rumble and hiss.

The app code is MIT licensed. These recordings keep their own licenses, listed
below. Files marked CC BY-SA are shared under the same license. The one CC BY-NC
file (`chick.mp3`) may not be used commercially. Swap it out before any
commercial use.
